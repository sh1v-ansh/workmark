RECOMMENDATION

Instrument Sans, one family, 600 for headings

Fallback stack: Instrument Sans, system-ui, sans-serif

It is the closest thing on Google Fonts to what you liked about Stripe. A neo-grotesque with narrow apertures and almost no personality — which sounds like a criticism and is the entire point. Nothing about the letterforms competes with the words, which is what makes a dense screen feel calm.

Its 600 is a real semibold, not a light bold. That is the specific thing Schibsted lacks: the gap between 600 and 700 is small there, so you reach for 700 and get more weight than you wanted. Here 600 holds a heading on its own.

One family, not two. Stripe sets everything in one face, and dropping Hanken removes a decision from every new component — no more “is this a display or a body string?”. It also cuts a font request off every page load.

If you want to keep more warmth, Onest is the humanist option and the closest in temperature to what you have now. If you want the most plainspoken thing on the list, Public Sans was drawn for government forms and it shows — in a good way.
